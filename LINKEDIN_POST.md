Safe to Send · Ep 15 — Your playbook scored 0/100

Here's a real-looking pre-send checklist:
"Check the email properly. Make sure the links work, the prices are right etc. Use best judgement on timing."

A new colleague would ask five questions. A model asks none. It guesses, and guesses differently each time.

So I built a linter for ops playbooks and AI skill files. It checks for four parts every executable playbook needs (when to use, what it must never do, numbered steps, what done looks like) and flags vague words: properly, as needed, etc, ensure, best judgement.

The checklist above scored 0/100. The rewrite scored 100/100 and took ten minutes.

The hardest section to write wasn't the steps. It was "Never".

Prompt engineering for ops isn't clever wording. It's writing your playbook so precisely that anyone, human or model, follows it the same way.
